What it does

03 modes / one app

Face detection

A Haar cascade — the same classic technique that first put face detection in point-and-shoot cameras — tracks your face every frame, drawn as a live bounding box.

GENDER  M/F
AGE      ±yrs
MOOD   8 states

Age, gender & mood

Two on-device neural networks read the detected face — one estimates age and gender, the other classifies expression into eight emotions.

Finger drawing

Hand-tracking finds 21 points on your hand, 30+ times a second. Spread your fingers to draw, pinch to lift the pen — five colors, plus an eraser. Draw over your camera feed, or switch to a full-screen blank notepad page instead.

How the drawing works

landmarks #4 & #8
  1. 01

    Find the hand

    MediaPipe's Hand Landmarker locates 21 points per hand from the raw camera frame — no glove, no marker, no special lighting.

  2. 02

    Measure the pinch

    The app watches the distance between landmark 4 (thumb tip) and landmark 8 (index tip). Close together means pinched.

  3. 03

    Draw or pause

    Fingers apart draws a line from your last position to your current one; a pinch lifts the pen without drawing.
